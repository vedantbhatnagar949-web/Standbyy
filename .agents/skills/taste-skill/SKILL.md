# Taste Skill

Design is not just how it looks, but the discerning restraint of what is left out. Taste is the curation of beauty, subtlety, and authentic luxury.

## Principles of Elevated Taste

### 1. Editorial Restraint
- **The Power of Space**: Empty negative space is a luxury material. Never crowd a design. Allow monumental elements room to breathe.
- **Rejection of Gimmicks**: Avoid cheap rainbow gradients or over-saturated cartoon colors. Use curated, deep jewel tones (Imperial Amber, Royal Sapphire, Obsidian Noir, Amethyst Dusk) that feel grounded and timeless.

### 2. High-Order Typography
- **Harmonious Contrast**: Pair a monumental, authoritative headline with an intimate, organic secondary voice (e.g. geometric bold with bespoke cursive or editorial serif).
- **Kerning & Tracking**: Monumental titles require wide, confident tracking (`letterSpacing: 4.0` to `10.0`). Body text requires relaxed line height (`height: 1.4` to `1.6`) for reading ease.

### 3. Materiality & Tactility
- Treat digital UI elements as physical luxury objects: polished obsidian crystal, brushed bronze/gold, refractive liquid glass, and velvety dark matter.
- Specular highlights should respond to an imagined light source consistently across the entire composition.
